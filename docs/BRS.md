# Business Requirements Specification – PumpPrognosis

| ID | Requirement |
|----|-------------|
| BR-1 | Estimate remaining useful life (days) of a pump from its health-index history. |
| BR-2 | Provide an interval around the estimate with a stated coverage target (90 %). |
| BR-3 | Raise a maintenance alert that gives a usable lead time while keeping early false alarms low. |
| BR-4 | Report behaviour when wear conditions differ from those used for calibration. |
| BR-5 | State plainly that evidence is synthetic until field data are available. |

Out of scope: real-plant integration, CMMS connectors, any claim on real equipment.
